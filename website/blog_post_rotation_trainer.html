Learn Your Rotations with Our New Interactive Rotation Trainer

Posted by Greater Orlando Volleyball Club

Ever freeze up trying to remember who should be where after a side-out? We just launched the Rotation Trainer -- a free, interactive tool that walks you through every rotation of the three most common offensive systems, right in your browser.

No login, no app to install, nothing to print out. Pick a system, pick a rotation, and watch exactly where every role lines up on the court -- whether you're serving or receiving serve.

What it covers

All 3 systems, all 6 rotations

5-1, 6-2, and 4-2 offenses, each with all six rotations mapped out -- including exactly which setter is running the offense (and when they're penetrating from the back row to do it).

Serving vs. Serve Receive

Toggle between the strict overlap-rule serving formation and a realistic serve-receive passing shape, so you can see how the court changes the instant the ball is live.

Libero & Defensive Specialist subs

Switch on libero substitutions, add a defensive specialist, or turn subs off entirely to see every rotation play out exactly how your team runs it.

Seam coverage

Highlight the passing seams between receivers and watch the coverage shrink as neighboring passers close the gap -- a great way to actually see why servers target those spots.

It's built for players, coaches, and anyone who's ever gotten yelled at for standing in the wrong zone -- whether you're brand new to rotations or just want a quick refresher before a match.
