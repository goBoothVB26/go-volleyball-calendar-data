GOVC Holiday Campaign How-tos

Create A Fundraising Team
  1. Go to https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/peer-to-peer/govc-holiday-fundraiser
  2. Click “Fundraise”
  3. Make sure “Create a team” is selected
  4. Input your team name and your details
  5. Make sure “I will be joining the team” is selected
  6. Click Create team
  7. On your team page, copy the share link to share your page with friends and family or edit your campaign to customize your page!
Join A Team
  1. Go to https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/peer-to-peer/govc-holiday-fundraiser
  2. Click “Fundraise”
  3. Select “Join a team”
  4. Select your desired team
  5. Input your details and click “Join team”
  6. Click “Share campaign” to copy your link and share your donation page with family and friends!
Donate directly to GOVC
  1. Go to https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/peer-to-peer/govc-holiday-fundraiser
  2. To donate directly click “Donate”
  3. Click the GOVC campaign
  4. Choose your donation type and amount, then click “Continue”
  5. Complete the donation form and click “Donate”
